# AREA-POLY-RAT1 Risks

## Correctness Risk

Polynomial/rational algorithms can appear simple while depending on coefficient domains, factor multiplicity, variable ordering, and denominator constraints. The highest near-term risk is widening rational integration before repeated/quadratic factor readiness is typed and tested.

## Honesty Risk

Studying seven powerful mirrors can create broad feature-pressure. Calcwiz must say that it is learning from these systems, not promising parity with FriCAS, SymPy, Maxima, SageMath, Giac/XCAS, SymEngine, or GeoGebra.

## Architecture Risk

The dangerous path is algorithm leakage: putting square-free, repeated-factor, or quadratic partial-fraction logic directly into calculus. That would make rational integration work but weaken reusable algebra ownership.

## Licensing Risk

The mirrors have mixed licenses, including GPL-family projects and mixed GeoGebra licensing. This study cites paths and extracts ideas only. Direct code reuse remains disallowed unless a separate source/license review is explicitly opened.

## Mitigation

- Choose `POLY-RAT-CORE1` before `INT-RAT2`.
- Keep source mirrors static-only and ignored.
- Add readiness facts and stop reasons before visible wins.
- Treat resultants/Grobner as a separate future area study.
- Promote benchmark families only after Calcwiz-native support exists.
