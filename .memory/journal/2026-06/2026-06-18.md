## Workspace Tabs UX Decision: Side Panels And Full Pages

- [agent: codex | model: gpt-5.5] Recorded the product distinction that tabs do not remove quick side panels.
- Quick side panels remain valuable for while-working essentials: recent History, pending/running job glance, quick replay/copy/stop, common Settings toggles, Vars, and developer diagnostics.
- Dedicated tab pages are the future home for deeper management surfaces: full History/Records, full Settings, richer Variables manager, Guide pages if needed, and future Graphing/Spreadsheet surfaces.
- Current committed History remains global and computation-record based. Future Graphing/Spreadsheet/artifact records should not be forced into the current `HistoryEntry` schema if they need their own saved-work or artifact manager.
- The Settings side panel should stay small and fast; a future full Settings tab can carry richer categorized settings without cramming the right inspector.
- Recorded the stronger architectural reason: tabs are the app's full-surface layer, not only a multi-calculator convenience.
- Without that role, future rich surfaces would be forced into cramped side panels, the MathEditor/result shell, scattered special routes/modals, or artificially small feature shapes.
