## Closeout Statement

The agreed Rubi Tier I track is closed for Calcwiz's current exact-rational plus target-free symbolic scope after `RUBI-TIER1-CLOSEOUT-GATED1`.

This means the high-frequency classroom families agreed with the user are now covered by bounded internal rules, route-local proofs, exact backcheck where available, visible `Valid When` facts, and controlled unsupported stops. It does not mean Calcwiz implements the full Rubi rule table or a Risch/Risch-Norman decision procedure.

## Covered Tier-I Surface

- Power, affine power, polynomial expansion, and polynomial-factor by-parts feeder cases.
- Variable-aware indefinite integration with selected integration variable and target-free symbolic coefficients.
- Exact-rational and target-free symbolic affine trig/exp/log primitives, positive symbolic-base exponentials, and bounded polynomial-times exp/trig/log by-parts.
- Derivative-present substitution families including binomial and reciprocal-binomial forms.
- Exact-rational rational partial fractions through repeated linear factors, one/two bounded irreducible quadratic groups, completed-square quadratics, repeated quadratic reciprocals, and numerator-over-quadratic families.
- Exact-rational affine trig derivative products, parity powers, tan/sec and cot/csc reductions, and two-factor product-to-sum support.
- Exact-rational inverse-trig affine variants and the three standard exact-rational affine trig-substitution radical families.
- Reliability surface: lazy guarded Compute Engine fallback, target-free selected-variable polynomial direct integration, MathLive-safe copy result behavior, and vertically stacked `Valid When` facts.

## Deferrals

- Full Rubi rule-table coverage and tiered dynamic Rubi imports.
- Broader symbolic assumptions, parameter-field algebra, symbolic discriminant/branch analysis, and complex-principal symbolic bases.
- Full Euler substitutions and broader algebraic radical decision procedures.
- Arcsec-style reciprocal-root readback until branch/domain facts are stronger.
- Risch/Risch-Norman, transcendental Risch, and non-elementary certificates.
- Research-grade special functions, elliptic/hyperelliptic reductions, and arbitrary CAS recursion.

## Risk Notes

- New symbolic or radical families must keep the proof/fact rule: no numeric-confidence adoption for symbolic coefficient or branch-sensitive results.
- Compute Engine fallback must remain lazy and guarded; it is not a routing substitute for parameter-heavy selected-variable cases.
- Display/readback polish must not create public schema changes unless a later milestone explicitly approves them.
