## What Is Achieved Now

- List markers and paragraph styles communicate familiar visual meaning while preserving selection, nesting, semantic headings, and undo/redo.
- Structural insertion ends pristine onboarding, and one-click content editing can target the nearest structured block without stealing the text caret.
- Inspector visibility has auto, manual-empty, pinned, and collapsed behavior with side-correct independent rails.
- Version 6 academic containers and Sections persist accent, collapsibility override, and compatible current collapsed state through strict validation and continuous migration.

## Manual App Steps

1. Open a new Notebook, manually restore the Inspector, and confirm its empty guidance appears on the right without opening Outline.
2. Open Bullet, Number, and paragraph-style menus. Confirm familiar marker sequences and visibly distinct Normal, Heading 1, Heading 2, and Heading 3 previews.
3. Insert an academic container and a Section. Confirm both onboarding prompts disappear immediately, then single-click inside their content and confirm the caret remains usable while the nearest shell appears in Inspector.
4. In an academic-container Inspector, set number and label, cycle all six accent presets, choose a low-contrast custom color, reset to Automatic, and change its type. Confirm the warning does not block the custom value and explicit appearance/collapsibility overrides survive type changes.
5. Toggle Collapsible, then use only the block-header chevron to collapse and expand. Disable Collapsible while collapsed and confirm the content expands and the persisted collapse state clears.
6. Repeat title, accent, and collapse checks for a Section; confirm the Outline chevron navigates but does not change persisted collapse.
7. Close and restore Outline and Inspector independently at desktop width, then repeat from each narrow drawer around 1,100 pixels.
8. Check 80% and 130% UI scale plus high contrast, and open Math Authoring while a structured-block menu or drawer has been used.

## Expected Results

- Inspector controls match the selected academic container or Section, empty/manual/pinned behavior remains predictable, and the editor selection is not lost.
- Accents affect the left rail/border, icon, and subtle header tint only; bodies remain neutral and forced colors remain legible.
- Hint and Answer are automatically collapsible, other academic containers are not, and Sections are; explicit overrides remain valid and incompatible collapsed state never persists.
- Menus, drawers, and Math Authoring do not overlap or cross side ownership at supported widths and scales.
